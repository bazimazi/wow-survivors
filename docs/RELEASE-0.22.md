## Design recorded before implementation

Continue the animation roadmap with melee, projectile and telegraphed attack gestures for every outdoor/dungeon creature, including elites, all guardians and code-drawn Mr. Smite. Reuse the original atlases, six combat poses and padded texture mesh. Add original spider, earthborer and spirit combat weights so their attacks suit their silhouettes. Source PNGs stay unchanged. Death cycles and new directional illustrations remain later work.

Add an optional enemy presentation callback after a contact hit is accepted (including shield absorption), a ranged projectile is actually launched or a boss attack registers warning hazards. Immune/out-of-range/dead actors, saturated projectile/hazard pools, hazard damage ticks and summons do not generate redundant cues. Preserve existing hit events, immunity windows, encounter timers, damage, resources and RNG. Compare simulation with and without observation across zones and boss patterns.

Keep weak gesture state in the renderer. Ordinary responses last 0.42 seconds; boss preparation spans the published warning, followed by a short release/recovery. Overlapping ordinary attacks finish their current gesture, while telegraphs can interrupt. Aim fixes gesture facing. Global pause/choices/recovery/results/focus loss hold poses. Individual freezing holds the visible pose; thaw samples the actual combat clock so already-resolved warnings cannot replay. Clock rewinds/expiry, dead actors and disabling motion clear stale responses. Charge/teleport rendering uses actual actor coordinates, with the locomotion baseline handling jumps.

Retain the 128-frame/128-pending/one-build creature cache and independent 192-frame/two-build character cache. Give boss misses priority for alternate builds when ordinary misses are waiting, preserving FIFO within each group. A full queue can replace its newest ordinary miss to admit a boss request; old ordinary requests retain service. Cold misses still use the original illustration. Existing animation settings and live reduced motion apply.

Verify cue guards, every current creature/guardian style mapping, warning alignment, pause/freeze/expiry/reset, positive finite padded geometry, native pixels for all 35 crops, missing-art/Smite fallbacks, charge/teleport and death cancellation, priority fairness/storage/construction limits and a real 400-enemy moving/attacking scene. Inspect contact sheets and phone/desktop warning captures, run the full regression, update roadmap/art/validation notes and preserve historical artifacts.

## Implemented behavior

All **36 illustrated creature identities**, elites, **15 guardians** and code-drawn Mr. Smite use six-pose strike, shoot or cast responses. Contact cues follow accepted hits, including shield absorption. The four existing ranged creature types notify only after launching a projectile. Boss cues follow registered hazards and carry the published warning duration and attack pattern. Immunity, failed range checks, full projectile/hazard pools, summons and lingering damage ticks produce no redundant cue. The optional callback leaves existing events and combat/progression rules compatible.

Ordinary responses last **0.42 seconds**. Guardians gather/draw back through their warning, then release and recover over **0.28 seconds**. Telegraphs interrupt ordinary gestures; repeated ordinary hits finish the current response. Aim fixes facing and observed locomotion continues underneath. Original spider, earthborer and spirit combat weights join the existing rigs; missing-image drawings and Smite use restrained lean/reach transforms. Source illustrations are unchanged.

Weak renderer state holds through pause, choices, recovery/results, focus loss and individual freezing. Incoming accepted actions during individual freezing leave the held pose intact. Thaw reads the actual combat clock and discards expired responses, including warnings that resolved during freezing. Rewinds, death and motion disabling clear gestures. Live reduced motion and the existing Character animation setting apply. Charges and Shadow Port draw from actual actor coordinates; the locomotion baseline resets jumps. Damage, warning geometry and encounter movement retain their timing.

The creature queue retains **128 cached frames**, **128 pending requests** and at most **one construction per render**. Boss combat requests get alternate service while ordinary requests wait, with FIFO order within each group. Full queues admit boss requests by replacing the newest ordinary miss, preserving older waiting work. The separate character cache retains its 192-frame/two-build limits. Cold misses draw the original illustration and disabling motion cancels queued construction. Save schema/key and first-free-port startup remain compatible.

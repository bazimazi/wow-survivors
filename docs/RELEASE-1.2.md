# Release 1.2 — Class identity

Space now dashes. C uses your class signature. Shift remains a dash shortcut. Dash works while standing still, uses your facing when there is no movement input, locks its direction for the burst, grants brief invulnerability, and cannot bypass cooldowns or game pauses. Controller A / Cross remains the signature; B / Circle remains dash. Touch has separate signature and dash buttons. P2 uses Enter for the signature and Backspace for dash.

## Nine different combat loops

| Class   | Mechanical identity                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | What to practice                                                                   |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Warrior | Contact and Charge generate Rage. Rage Cleave spends up to 60 Rage in a frontal arc, knocks enemies away, and briefly protects you. Standing still grants armor.                                                                                                                                                              | Face the pack, Charge into position, then spend Rage.                              |
| Mage    | Standing still builds up to three Focus, adding up to 54% spell damage. Shatter freezes nearby enemies; a second Shatter during their freeze deals a larger burst. Blink moves 190 units instantly. Boss freezes are shorter.                                                                                                 | Find casting windows, freeze a pack, and exploit it before the freeze expires.     |
| Rogue   | Sinister Strike hits one victim and retains the combo target while it remains in range. Primary hits build five points; switching targets loses points. Eviscerate follows the combo mark even when another enemy is closer. Vanish Step breaks pursuit and ordinary ranged attacks and empowers the next attack or finisher. | Stay on your marked victim; choose between an early finisher and five-point burst. |
| Hunter  | Range beyond 150 units and stationary Aim improve shots; shooting inside melee range reduces damage. Trap & Command marks prey, improves the pet's damage against it, and lays a single-use frost trap.                                                                                                                       | Kite through the trap and stop safely to aim while your pet holds prey.            |
| Paladin | Primary hits build three seals. Judgement consumes seals to create a six-second consecrated ground that damages foes, heals nearby allies, and improves your attacks inside it.                                                                                                                                               | Build seals, judge where the pack will gather, and hold the ground.                |
| Priest  | Primary hits build five Grace. Sanctuary consumes Grace for party shields, a healing ward, and close-range displacement. Shield absorption restores Grace. Feather Step leaves a brief healing prayer.                                                                                                                        | Alternate offense with protection and keep injured allies near the ward.           |
| Shaman  | C cycles Earth, Fire and Storm totems. Three independent twelve-second territories slow/protect, burn, or strike three nearby foes. Replacing an element removes its previous totem. Placement fans around the caster so all three remain visible.                                                                            | Build a totem circle, then bring enemies into its territory.                       |
| Warlock | Shadow hits curse enemies for eight seconds. Life Tap trades 15% maximum health for Mana and six seconds of empowerment. Cursed kills build three shards; C spends shards for Soul Harvest damage and healing.                                                                                                                | Maintain curses; trade health deliberately and recover it through souls.           |
| Druid   | C cycles Moonkin, Cat, and Bear, preserving separate Mana/Energy/Rage reserves. Moonkin uses ranged nature magic. Cat spends Energy on faster close-range claws and moves faster. Bear swipes packs, spends Rage for empowered swipes, slows foes, and gains armor. Caster attacks and healing require Moonkin.               | Change forms to suit range, escape routes, and pack pressure.                      |

Every expedition launched by the app enables the new class combat system. Independent per-actor state keeps co-op points, curses, forms, reserves, traps, and totems separate. Party shields and healing support both living heroes. Ground effects expire, clear between dungeon rooms, and disappear when their owner falls. The engine retains the previous combat mode for existing diagnostic fixtures that omit `classCombat`.

Hunter shots and pets prioritize marked prey in range. Party kills clear combo/target marks immediately and grant soul shards to a living warlock whose curse is still active. Signature buttons show unavailable states when resources, combo points, target distance, health costs, or cooldowns prevent casting. P2’s compact HUD includes its own class meter state.

## Presentation and controls

Nine authored Canvas effect families use different shapes and trajectories: blade arcs, frost crystals, knife cuts, arrows and target reticles, holy crosses, priest wings, branching lightning, fel sigils, and leaves. Class-specific body choreography accompanies accepted attacks. Projectiles have their own class silhouettes rather than a shared colored line. Target-bound combo pips, hunter marks, and curse glyphs show what the mechanics are acting on. Earth, Fire, and Storm have distinct totem shapes and overlapping visible territories.

The camp describes each class's combat loop. A live meter and expandable instructions sit with the expedition HUD, with scroll limits on small screens. Dynamic signature labels show the next totem, next form, or Life Tap/Soul Harvest. Druid's resource label and bar color change with its form. UI, keyboard, controller, touch, pause guards, and repeated-key guards use the same engine actions. Class casts and signatures use nine synthesized audio voices; no external audio service is required.

Cat and Moonkin use the original generated atlas at [`public/art/druid-forms.png`](../public/art/druid-forms.png), animated with the quadruped and heavy rigs respectively. Bear uses the existing illustrated companion atlas. All assets remain local. Full generation provenance and the final prompt are in [the art record](ART-1.2.md).

## Research and adaptation

Blizzard's [original manual](https://assets.blz-contentstack.com/v3/assets/blt3452e3b114fab0cd/blt2e9295db02a222fc/6025bcbb6968b53d529edb2a/media_manual_classic_enUS.pdf), printed pages 70–86, describes class-specific resources, rogue finishers, druid forms, totem territories, hunter pets and ranged play, and the warlock's health/Mana and soul relationships. Blizzard's [Classic restoration discussion](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/22646759/restoring-history-creating-wow-classic-panel-recap) explains why target-bound combo points and resource timing change combat decisions. These references informed distinct player decisions rather than numerical spell swaps.

Focus, Grace, three stored seals, the three-totem cycle, Soul Harvest, the simplified form cycle, all dash variants, and all numeric values are original survivor-game adaptations. They are not a recreation of Vanilla class rotations. No game artwork or sound was extracted from Blizzard assets.

## Validation

`npm test`, `npm run build`, `npm run format:check`, and `npm run check:repo` verify the implementation. `tests/class-combat.test.ts` covers real damage/resource behavior, control guards, target switches, a second Shatter, range/Aim, trap consumption, party shields, anchored effects, health costs, form reserves, pursuit decoys, and downed owners. `tests/e2e/class-combat.spec.ts` exercises real keys and phone pointers, all nine class visuals, reduced motion, disabled particles, and form/resource changes. Existing animation, controller, travel, progression, and complete expedition regressions remain part of the browser suite.

Final evidence: **504 unit tests**, **249 distinct browser cases verified through the complete sweep and affected rechecks**, **65/65 final affected browser cases**, and **81/81 completed seeded balance diagnostics with no timeouts**. Full records and practical limits are in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).

Run `npx tsx scripts/class-balance.ts` for 81 seeded starter-build diagnostics across all nine classes, three challenge settings, and three seeds. Its pilot makes different signature and movement decisions for each class. The report is saved to `output/class-balance.json`. Automated pilots check functioning and pacing; human testing is still needed to assess enjoyment, readability under pressure, form-switching costs, and the desire to replay. Use the local playtest protocol from [release 1.1](RELEASE-1.1.md).

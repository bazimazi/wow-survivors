# v0.23 — Death presentation

## Design recorded before implementation

Continue the animation roadmap with six authored death poses for all illustrated creatures, guardians, nine heroes and Bear Form. Reuse the original atlas crops and padded mesh; code-drawn Smite and unavailable-image fallbacks share the collapse transform. Biped/robe/heavy actors fall to the side, quadrupeds/spiders/earthborers settle down and spirits dissolve. Source PNGs stay unchanged. Companions have no health/death rule and receive no invented death cue.

An optional engine callback reports each actual lethal enemy hit before removal/rewards/guardian room cleanup, and actual player defeat before the existing end event. Nonlethal hits, repeated damage, victory and healthy manual returns produce no death cue. Preserve immediate pickups, rewards, room transitions, results, settlement and combat RNG; verify same-seed observation is inert.

The renderer copies defeated actors into bounded presentation storage: at most 64 enemy snapshots plus one hero. Preserve guardians ahead of ordinary casualties when a large kill fills storage. Enemy visuals collapse and fade over 0.75 seconds, without collision, targeting, status markers or health bars. A defeated hero holds the final fallen pose behind the existing results. A separate, explicitly advanced presentation clock lets death visuals finish behind recovery/results while combat remains stopped. Pause, choices, focus loss and hidden documents hold presentation. Clamp delayed frames; additional redraws do not advance time. Dungeon stage changes and combat clock rewinds discard old snapshots. Animation disabling/live reduced motion discard enemy trails and show a static fallen hero; re-enabling cannot replay old deaths.

Retain existing creature/character cache limits and boss-priority service. Test lethal/duplicate/nonlethal guards, every creature and guardian, all hero classes/Bear Form, defeat versus return/victory, room clearing, snapshot independence, capacity/priority, pause/focus/clock/stage lifecycle, reduced motion and missing-art fallbacks. Check finite padded non-folding geometry, native atlas pixels and contact sheets, desktop/phone results/recovery, mass kills, unchanged simulation/RNG and full regression. Record measured evidence after implementation.

## Implemented behavior

Six authored poses now animate actual death for all **36 illustrated enemy identities**, elites, **15 guardians**, code-drawn Smite, all **nine heroes** and Bear Form. Original padded mesh weights droop the textures; ground-anchored transforms tip humanoids/heavy creatures sideways, settle low silhouettes and shrink/dissolve spirits. Ground shadows remain grounded. Missing-image drawings share the transforms. Source PNGs remain unchanged.

The optional callback fires once after lethal enemy damage marks an actor dead, before drops/rewards and room clearing. Actual hero defeat reports before the existing end event; nonlethal/duplicate hits, victory and healthy returns do not. Renderer snapshots copy defeated actors independently of gameplay storage, removing flashes, guard/freeze/DoT markers and health bars. Observed creature facing is retained. Enemy storage caps at **64**, retaining guardians ahead of ordinary casualties; one separate hero snapshot holds its final fallen pose.

Enemy visuals finish over **0.75 seconds** using explicit presentation delta. They can finish behind immediately displayed recovery/results without advancing combat, rewards or RNG. Pause, choices, focus loss and hidden documents hold presentation; frame deltas cap at 0.05 seconds and additional redraws advance nothing. Stage changes and clock rewinds discard snapshots. Animation disabling and live reduced motion remove enemy trails and show a static fallen hero; re-enabling cannot replay old deaths.

Existing creature cache limits (128 retained, 128 pending, one construction per render), guardian priority and the independent character cache (192 retained, two constructions per render) remain. Cold frames still use source art plus the collapse transform. Save schema/key, gameplay/progression rules, event compatibility and first-free-port startup remain compatible. Companions have no death mechanic and receive no invented cue. New directional illustration and physical-device feel remain later animation work.

The following notes preserve earlier balancing context.

`npm run balance` runs an explicit movement/upgrade policy across three deterministic seeds for each class. Initial measurements showed faster first upgrades for ranged classes and slower collection for melee. The pickup radius was increased, and melee classes receive an additional pickup bonus. Introductory Elwynn is intentionally forgiving. The boss was expanded from a simple ground telegraph into a longer encounter with reinforcements, a projectile fan and a second-phase ground pattern.

The script is a diagnostic policy that knows world state and avoids threats. Its win rate must not be treated as a human win rate or evidence that all content has final balance. ## Current limits

- Equipment covers ten slots and 154 items, with an acquisition guide for the forty new additions. Wrists, rings, off-hands, dual wielding, durability, soulbinding and personal item instances remain later design work. Existing enchantments support four slots. Automated equipped profiles do not establish human acquisition time or final difficulty.

- Class trainers provide two additional techniques per class with original survival-game learning gates and values. A complete vanilla rank catalog, party healing, combo points, weapon queues, threat and stationary channels remain future systems. Automated diagnostics sample specific prepared builds; the Hunter tradeoff above needs human testing.

The 0.2 advanced diagnostic used one primary specialization plus seven points in a secondary tree, all six equipment slots, three healing supplies and one bomb. Across the 27 class/path cases in Tirisfal, 19 completed the expedition and eight died. This uses one seed per specialization, so path and seed are confounded; it is a combat/progression smoke check, not a ranking of specializations. It also does not measure human difficulty. The initial partial-loadout sample completed 18 of 27 cases. Further balance work should use multiple seeds per build and human sessions, especially for resource management and collecting XP under pressure.

- Single-player local browser game; no cloud sync or multiplayer.
- Compact class trees and a small initial equipment/recipe catalog, rather than the full Classic catalog.
- Class trials use original objectives with existing combat encounters; dedicated class quest maps, quests from the original game and additional spell unlocks are future content. Equipment enchantments use twelve static-stat formulas on four slots, with one shared owned copy per item.
- Static illustrated sprites with movement bobbing and facing changes; authored animation cycles are future work.
- Environmental props are scenery; they do not block movement. Combat uses a finite world boundary.
- The Deadmines selects three bosses with original solo attacks and rewards. Other original bosses, quests, the cannon sequence, a full navigable dungeon map and cooperative encounters are future content. Human sessions are needed to assess dungeon difficulty and reward pacing.
- Cleared zones persist independently of recent history. Older saves infer them only from retained victories; a Westfall victory already trimmed from history requires another clear to establish dungeon access.
- Westfall and Tirisfal have distinct palettes, enemies, durations and bosses, and use color-treated variants of the initial camp landscape illustration.
- Recipes cover skill requirements from 1 to 225, including three reputation patterns and seven specialized crafting paths. All six crafting trades plus Cooking and First Aid can practice through 300 with rank training. The four-grade material economy supports every gathering trade through 300. More recipes and profession quests remain future content. Human sessions are needed to assess trainer fees, material costs and new equipment balance.
- Reputation thresholds and repeatable reward pacing are compressed original designs. Human testing is still needed to assess long-term faction progression, quartermaster equipment balance and crafting resource costs.
- Touch movement and ability buttons are included; browser integration coverage focuses on keyboard gameplay and responsive layouts.
- Browser storage can be removed by browser cleanup. Save export/import is provided; an unfinished active expedition is not resumed after reload.

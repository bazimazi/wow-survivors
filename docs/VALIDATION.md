The following notes preserve earlier balancing context.

`npm run balance` runs an explicit movement/upgrade policy across three deterministic seeds for each class. Initial measurements showed faster first upgrades for ranged classes and slower collection for melee. The pickup radius was increased, and melee classes receive an additional pickup bonus. Introductory Elwynn is intentionally forgiving. The boss was expanded from a simple ground telegraph into a longer encounter with reinforcements, a projectile fan and a second-phase ground pattern.

The script is a diagnostic policy that knows world state and avoids threats. Its win rate must not be treated as a human win rate or evidence that all content has final balance. Additional human testing is needed for input feel, build diversity and Westfall/Tirisfal difficulty. Changes to mana/energy costs are included in the final verification pass.

Wrists, rings, off-hands, dual wielding, durability, soulbinding and personal item instances remain later design work. Existing enchantments support four slots. Automated equipped profiles do not establish human acquisition time or final difficulty.

- Class trainers provide two additional techniques per class with original survival-game learning gates and values. A complete vanilla rank catalog, party healing, combo points, weapon queues, threat and stationary channels remain future systems. Automated diagnostics sample specific prepared builds; the Hunter tradeoff above needs human testing.

The 0.2 advanced diagnostic used one primary specialization plus seven points in a secondary tree, all six equipment slots, three healing supplies and one bomb. This uses one seed per specialization, so path and seed are confounded; it is a combat/progression smoke check, not a ranking of specializations. It also does not measure human difficulty. Further balance work should use multiple seeds per build and human sessions, especially for resource management and collecting XP under pressure.
